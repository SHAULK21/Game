# Story introduction and resident buyout

New characters see five illustrated story cards before registration. Existing characters bypass the introduction. Completion/skip is stored per Telegram account on this device; changing device or clearing browser data can show it again. The story is available in Russian and Ukrainian and does not grant quest progress or change class mechanics.

The market has Knights (existing player listings), Local residents (instant buyout), and Basic shop. Residents pay floor(30% of the normal unit sellPrice), minimum 1 gold for a nonzero-price item, multiplied by selected quantity. There is no listing tax or energy charge. Locked, equipped and clan-bound items are excluded. Server-owned items use canonical database prices and quantities. Local items use the existing client-authoritative inventory model. Buyout receipts are replayable after a lost response; account epoch is checked while the player is locked in the transaction. No new schema migration is required.
