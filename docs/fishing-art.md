# Fishing artwork

Original artwork made for this repository with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The uploaded reference video informed the interaction idea, not the artwork or layout.

Production files: `public/assets/fishing/river.webp` and twelve fish illustrations in that directory. Background exported at 1024 px wide; inventory fish tiles exported at 192 × 170 px. All visible controls and game information are native UI, not painted into an image.

## Background prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background illustration for the fishing page of an original medieval fantasy RPG, a hand-painted living bestiary book. Create a wide landscape painting 1536x1024: quiet reed-lined river inlet at dawn, aged wooden landing stage entering from bottom left, a simple wooden fishing rod resting on the stage, one very small red-and-cork float on the water near the center; distant wooded hills and an old stone tower. Traditional gouache and detailed ink illustration, muted olive greens, aged parchment gold, dark brown wood, soft natural water reflections. Worn book illustration rather than neon videogame art. Empty scene without people. Keep the middle water area clear for a functional float animation. No interface, buttons, panels, symbols, borders, lettering or numbers. No purple neon, no imitation of the reference game's nocturnal scene.

## Fish atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production sprite atlas of 12 original fish inventory illustrations for a medieval painted bestiary RPG. Transparent background. Canvas 1536x1024, exactly four columns and three rows of evenly spaced equal-size cells; every fish entirely within its own cell with generous transparent padding. One centered side-view fish in each cell, all facing right, hand-painted gouache with fine dark ink outlines, realistic fish anatomy, slightly fantastical accents, muted natural colors, no glow. First row left to right: olive river perch with dark bands and red fins; bronze crucian carp; pearl-white moon carp. Second row: speckled brook trout; long green pike; blue silverfin carp. Third row: pale blue ice char; striped deepwater grayling; dark blue winter sturgeon. Fourth is NOT a row: third row includes only four fish, so full exact layout: row 1 perch, bronze carp, pearl moon carp, speckled trout; row 2 green pike, silverfin, ice char, grayling; row 3 winter sturgeon, dark brown cave catfish, red-and-gold rune koi, teal ancient tench. Exactly 12 fish, four per row. NO text, no labels, no inventory slots, no panels, no grid lines, no props, no decorations, no shadows outside fish. Each fish separate with no overlap.

The generated atlas used colored vignette backgrounds rather than alpha transparency. Individual painted tiles preserve those backgrounds and are used as inventory illustrations.
